# Interview Preparation Guide

This document contains curated knowledge about interview preparation strategies.
It will be indexed into the vector store for RAG-based retrieval.

## General Interview Tips

- Research the company thoroughly before the interview
- Practice the STAR method for behavioral questions
- Prepare 3-5 questions to ask the interviewer
- Review common data structures and algorithms for technical roles
- Practice system design for senior roles

## Technical Interview Preparation

- Review fundamentals of your primary programming language
- Practice coding problems on platforms like LeetCode or HackerRank
- Understand time and space complexity analysis
- Be prepared to discuss your past projects in depth
- Know the trade-offs of common architectural patterns

## Behavioral Interview Preparation

- Prepare stories about leadership, conflict resolution, and teamwork
- Use the STAR format: Situation, Task, Action, Result
- Be honest about failures and what you learned from them
- Show enthusiasm for the role and company mission
