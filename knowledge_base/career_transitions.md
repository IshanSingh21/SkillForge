# Career Transition Guide

This document contains curated knowledge about navigating career transitions.

## Planning a Career Transition

- Identify transferable skills from your current role
- Research the target role's requirements thoroughly
- Build a portfolio of relevant projects
- Network with professionals in your target field
- Consider transitional roles that bridge your current and target positions

## Common Career Transitions in Tech

- Frontend to Full-Stack: Learn backend frameworks and databases
- Developer to Engineering Manager: Develop leadership and communication skills
- Data Analyst to Data Scientist: Deepen statistical and ML knowledge
- QA to DevOps: Learn CI/CD, containerization, and cloud platforms
- Backend to ML Engineer: Study machine learning fundamentals and MLOps

## Building Your Transition Portfolio

- Create 3-5 projects demonstrating skills for the target role
- Write blog posts explaining concepts in the new domain
- Contribute to open source projects in the target area
- Get relevant certifications
- Update your resume to highlight transferable skills
