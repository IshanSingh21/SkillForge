# Natural Language Processing: Career Guide & Skill Progression

## Overview & Core Definition
Natural Language Processing (NLP) is the intersection of computer science, artificial intelligence, and linguistics concerned with enabling computers to understand, interpret, extract, process, and generate human language in text and speech forms.

## Fundamental Concepts & Theory
- **Classical Text Processing**: Tokenization, stemming, lemmatization, stop-word removal, n-grams, part-of-speech (POS) tagging, and named entity recognition (NER).
- **Text Representation**: Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency (TF-IDF), Bag of Words (BoW), Word2Vec, GloVe, FastText, and contextualized embeddings.
- **Transformer Architectures**: Scaled Dot-Product Self-Attention, Multi-Head Attention, Positional Encodings, Encoder-only (BERT, RoBERTa), Decoder-only (GPT-style), and Encoder-Decoder (T5, BART) paradigms.
- **Core NLP Tasks**: Text Classification, Sentiment Analysis, Named Entity Extraction, Question Answering, Machine Translation, Abstractive & Extractive Summarization, and Information Retrieval.
- **Evaluation Metrics**: BLEU, ROUGE, Exact Match (EM), F1-score, Perplexity, and semantic similarity metrics.

## Core Tools, Libraries & Frameworks
- **Classical NLP & Linguistics**: spaCy, NLTK, Stanza.
- **Modern Transformer Frameworks**: Hugging Face Transformers, Datasets, Tokenizers, Accelerate.
- **Dense Embedding Frameworks**: Sentence-Transformers, Gensim.
- **Vector Search & Indexing**: FAISS, ChromaDB, Qdrant, Milvus.

## Prerequisites & Foundational Knowledge
- **Core Python & Data Structures**: String manipulation, regular expressions (Regex), hash maps, matrix multiplication.
- **Deep Learning Fundamentals**: PyTorch basics, attention mechanism mechanics, sequence modeling, cross-entropy loss.
- **Linguistic Understanding**: Syntax, semantics, ambiguity handling, and token boundary considerations.

## Practical Projects & Portfolio Experience
1. **Resume & Job Requirement Matcher**: Semantic skill extractor using spaCy NER combined with Sentence-Transformers for semantic similarity matching.
2. **Domain-Specific Text Classifier**: Multi-label ticket routing or legal document categorization using fine-tuned RoBERTa.
3. **Information Extraction System**: Custom Named Entity Recognition (NER) pipeline trained with spaCy or Hugging Face to extract structured entities from unstructured contracts or resumes.

## Career Roles & Industry Demand
- **NLP Engineer**: Builds text processing, entity extraction, and semantic search pipelines.
- **Search & Relevance Engineer**: Optimizes retrieval algorithms, ranking models, and semantic discovery engines.
- **Conversational AI Engineer**: Designs dialogue agents, intent classifiers, and customer service automation systems.

## Interconnected Fields & Cross-Disciplinary Paths
- **Generative AI & LLMs**: Extends NLP concepts into massive autoregressive foundation models and instruction tuning.
- **Information Retrieval (IR)**: Traditional keyword search (BM25) fused with neural semantic search (dense retrieval).
- **Speech Processing (ASR/TTS)**: Acoustic models paired with language modeling for voice applications.

## Suggested Learning Progression
1. **Phase 1: Text Foundations**: Regex, NLTK, spaCy linguistic parsing, TF-IDF representations.
2. **Phase 2: Word & Sentence Embeddings**: Word2Vec, FastText, Sentence-Transformers (`all-MiniLM-L6-v2`), and Cosine Similarity.
3. **Phase 3: Transformer Models**: Fine-tuning BERT/RoBERTa using Hugging Face Transformers for classification and NER.
4. **Phase 4: Semantic Search & Retrieval**: Building vector indices with FAISS, dense passage retrieval, and hybrid search.
